## Architecture assessment before editing

- `missionData.ts` defines five stages, individual mission questions and fixed `finalQuestions`. `App` derives the current stage from the question, rather than routing to a separate Final Case application.
- `gameState.ts` owns answer validation, retry/assistance, cursor advancement and localStorage validation. Evidence is derived from `completed`, not a parallel unlock store.
- `QuestionCard`, `ImageChoice`, `ToolVisual` and `FeedbackPanel` already supply accessible bilingual tool cards, chosen-tool feedback and progressive evidence revelation.
- `InvestigationEvidence` also renders in the Notebook; reuse avoids separate versions of the same evidence. The existing Notebook knowledge cards represent observation tools, so blastema is a discoverable “more to find” note on evidence 02 rather than a new knowledge-card category.
- `images.ts` is the public specimen/evidence registry; `toolIllustrations.ts` registers the six tool SVGs. These are distinct systems. Final Case uses both through their existing renderers.
- `bi()` and locale-indexed `Text` remain the bilingual mechanism. No language/global-state library was introduced.
- Stage 04 has independent protein/membrane/fish-swimming tool decisions. Preserve it unchanged; the final case now adds connected observations and interpretation.

## Before and after

Before: mystery tissue → compound light/cell outlines → fluorescence/protein X → required TEM/mitochondrial detail → case closed.

After: injured zebrafish opening → three locked evidence slots → stereo/whole-fin time sequence → compound light/wound tissue → fluorescence/labeled proliferating cells → visible three-clue review and synthesis question → case solved → optional TEM.

| Investigation | Question and tool | Revealed evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Does the whole fin change over days? Stereo microscope. | D0/D3/D7/D14 outline series; fin regrowth, no invented measurements. |
| 2 | What are cells and tissue like near the wound? Compound light microscope. | Thin-section model, wound locator, cells in the regenerating region. Morphology alone does not identify proliferating cells. |
| 3 | Where are specifically labeled proliferating cells? Fluorescence microscope. | Aligned cellular view with a bright ring plus color on a subset of nuclei; defined EdU model explained in observation notes. |

Synthesis asks how the fin grows back: A remaining fin only stretches; B nearby cells multiply and rebuild tissue (correct); C wound covering alone restores the whole fin. Three evidence discoveries do not complete the mission until this question is resolved through the existing answer/assistance flow.

Bonus TEM uses the same QuestionCard/reducer/feedback components. It is inside a native disclosure on the solved screen, before the badge. A separate optional `bonus` progress record prevents it from affecting the main count or completion. Wrong answers, assistance, collection, reload, Notebook review and replay use existing patterns. Collection returns keyboard focus to the disclosure summary.

## Scientific wording

- Whole-fin regrowth, wound healing, cellular participation/proliferation and gradual tissue rebuilding are distinct observations/concepts.
- The main tissue evidence says many cells are present. Participation in regeneration is interpreted with research context; cell outlines alone cannot establish cell activity or fate.
- Blastema is described as a regenerating region containing cells from different origins, not identical multipotent stem cells.
- The fluorescence teaching model is defined as EdU incorporation during new DNA synthesis followed by fluorescent detection. It is a proliferation indicator, not direct observation of every division, all regeneration-related cells, or proof of causality.
- Fluorescent rings are an accessibility/diagram convention, not a claim that EdU physically forms a ring around nuclei. The adjacent caption and alternative text describe the teaching symbol.
- D0/D3/D7/D14 are explicitly illustrative time points, not a fixed regeneration timetable or measured dataset.
- Different observations may require separately prepared specimens. TEM reuses a general mitochondrial teaching diagram, not a measured ultrastructure image of this fish or a digital enlargement of the fluorescence field.
- The solved screen ends with choosing tools for questions and combining evidence. TEM explicitly answers a different question and never gates main completion.

Primary research checked for the content decisions:

- [Tu and Johnson, 2011: Fate restriction in the growing and regenerating zebrafish fin](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3096007/) — distinct lineage classes; does not support an identical generic-stem-cell model.
- [Stewart and Stankunas, 2012: Limited Dedifferentiation Provides Replacement Tissue during Zebrafish Fin Regeneration](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3370987/) — cellular contributions and proliferation in regeneration.
- [Genetic reprogramming of positional memory in a regenerating appendage](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6917923/) — blastemal proliferation, DNA synthesis and EdU-positive cells.

Layout validation therefore used live page interaction, accessibility/DOM inspection, rendered element dimensions, overflow checks and image-load checks. Original SVG artwork was separately raster-rendered and visually inspected. A final human screenshot/visual pass on physical mobile/tablet devices remains recommended; pixel-level browser screenshot QA is not claimed.

## Recommended manual path

1. `npm run dev` (or `npm run build` then `npm run preview`); start Detective Mission.
2. Complete stages 01–03. Stage 04 remains protein → fluorescence, fine membranes → electron, swimming adult fish → eyes.
3. Enter the fin case: injured fish and three locked slots. Confirm no future evidence titles are visible.
4. Choose a wrong tool, then stereo. Inspect D0/D3/D7/D14 and unlock 01. Expand its tracker thumbnail using keyboard/touch.
5. Continue to tissue; try a wrong tool, reload to check persistence, then choose compound light. Unlock 02 and optionally read about blastema.
6. Continue to labeled cells; try optical twice, then use assistance or choose fluorescence. Inspect aligned nuclei and marked subset; open specimen notes for EdU context.
7. Continue: three evidence cards are visible together. A/C should invite retry; B explains tissue rebuilding. Only then collect the badge.
8. Confirm the main case is complete without entering TEM. Optionally expand Bonus Investigation, retry once, choose TEM and collect. Reload and inspect the same bonus evidence in the Notebook.
9. Repeat in English and at 1366/768/390/320 px. Check wrapped text, keyboard focus, image size, disclosure targets and no horizontal page scrolling. Check the full mission route both collapsed and expanded.
10. Replay: main and bonus discoveries clear. Next Detective resets all collections and returns to the Chinese landing page.
